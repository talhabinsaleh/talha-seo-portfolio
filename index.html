About

I help businesses get found. That means research before opinions, links from sites people actually read, and websites fast enough to keep the visitors search sends them.

Who I am

I'm Talha Bin Saleh, an SEO specialist and WordPress developer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. At Yaaply Limited I work on client campaigns from the technical audit through on-page work to high-authority link outreach.

I also design and build complete WordPress and WooCommerce websites from scratch, with more than ten live so far. I'm finishing a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering too, so I can read the code behind an SEO problem and fix it myself.

Work I enjoy

I like the detective work: opening a competitor's backlink profile, finding out why they outrank a client, then building a list of sites worth contacting. A clean spreadsheet with a clear next step is very satisfying to me.

I also enjoy the moment a technical fix or a new page starts picking up impressions in Search Console. It's the proof that the plan worked.
